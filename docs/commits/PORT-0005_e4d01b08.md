## 2. Rationale & Defect Description

When a bot died and received a resurrection offer from another bot or player, it relied on packet event handling which often failed or missed the trigger, causing the bot to ignore the resurrect and auto-release. Querying bot->GetResurrector() and adding a high-relevance 'very often' trigger ensures bots reliably accept resurrection.
